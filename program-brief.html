Dimaag aur Machine
Program brief for school management
Nashik, Maharashtra · Academic year 2026–27

AI literacy, taught inside your school.

A two or three day workshop for Classes 9 to 12, conducted on school premises during school hours, with a school teacher present in every session. It is not a coding class and not exam preparation. It teaches students to use AI tools competently, judge what those tools produce, and understand where they must not be trusted.

The two modules

For students

Soch aur System™

Machine jawab de sakti hai. Sochna tumhe hai.  ·  Core — 2 days  ·  Applied — 3 days

  • How tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini actually work
  • Prompt engineering, and how to judge an answer critically
  • Where AI fails and hallucinates, and when not to trust it
  • Using AI to study rather than to shortcut thinking
  • Ethics, responsible use, online safety, AI career landscape
  • The three-day version adds one complete hands-on project
For teachers

Chalk aur Calibration™ — Subject Edition

Teacher wahi. Toolkit nayi.  ·  Department-wise  ·  2–6 hrs per subject

  • Turn an existing chapter into quizzes, flashcards, worksheets and revision material
  • Built around each teacher's own subject — Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Languages, Humanities
  • Output is reusable year after year, reducing fresh preparation
  • No prior technical background assumed

What each side is responsible for

The school provides

  • A venue and a time slot inside the timetable
  • Management approval for the batch
  • Communication to parents about enrolment
  • One teacher present during sessions, for supervision

We handle everything else

  • Curriculum, facilitation and all session materials
  • Certificates, co-signed by the school
  • All parent queries and concerns, directly — never routed through school staff
  • Devices where needed; sessions adapt to whatever is available

Teachers are not evaluated on program outcomes, are not held responsible for student performance during sessions, and are not expected to prepare anything beforehand. Students never use AI tools unsupervised, and there is no homework or after-school commitment.

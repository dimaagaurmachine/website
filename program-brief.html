Dimaag aur Machine
Program brief for school management
Nashik, Maharashtra · Academic year 2026–27

From AI users to AI builders. Class 8 se 12 tak.

Students use AI every day and mostly trust it blindly. This two or three day workshop, run inside school hours with a school teacher present, teaches Classes 8 to 12 to build with AI and to build AI: working in teams, each team creates a working AI agent for a real problem, without writing code. AI is now a life skill for every profession, not only for coders.

The two modules

For students

Soch aur System™

Machine jawab de sakti hai. Sochna tumhe hai.  ·  2 days or 3 days, the school chooses

  • Every team builds a working AI agent for a real problem, like North on our website
  • Build with AI: AI models and tools as building blocks; managing AI agents through a workflow
  • Build AI: how it works underneath, where it fails, and where use turns into misuse
  • 30-minute cycles of learning a concept and building with it, on one system throughout
  • 3 days adds advanced modules and a judged 5-minute pitch per team, with prizes
  • Classes 8–10 and 11–12 take separate workshops; no coding in either
For teachers

Chalk aur Calibration™ — Subject Edition

Teacher wahi. Toolkit nayi.  ·  Department-wise  ·  2–6 hrs per subject

  • Turn an existing chapter into quizzes, flashcards, worksheets and revision material
  • Built around each teacher's own subject — Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Languages, Humanities
  • Output is reusable year after year, reducing fresh preparation
  • No prior technical background assumed

What each side is responsible for

The school provides

  • Access to the computer lab and the AV room
  • Two or three days, 4 hours each, in school hours
  • One or two teachers present in every session
  • Its channels to parents, distribution of our approved flyers, and fee collection

We handle everything else

  • Program design and all teaching, on our own build platform
  • Headphones for every team where the lab has none; all marketing material
  • Answers to every parent question about the program
  • Certificates, prizes, the pitch event, photos and a highlight video for the school

Teachers are not evaluated on program outcomes, are not held responsible for student performance during sessions, and are not expected to prepare anything beforehand. Students never sign up for any app, never enter personal details into an AI tool, and are photographed only with written parental consent. There is no homework or after-school commitment.

Enrolment is voluntary. There is no subscription, no material sold afterwards, and no follow-up program to decline later.

Dimaag aur Machine
Program brief for school management
Nashik, Maharashtra · Academic year 2026–27

From AI users to AI builders, inside your school.

A two or three day workshop for Classes 8 to 12, run in the school’s computer lab during school hours, with a school teacher present in every session. Working in teams, students build a working AI study assistant for a chapter of their own syllabus, without writing code. They learn how AI produces answers, where it goes wrong, and how to test it.

The two modules

For students

Soch aur System™

Machine jawab de sakti hai. Sochna tumhe hai.  ·  Core — 2 days  ·  Applied — 3 days

  • Every team builds Padhai Saathi, a voice AI study companion for one chapter of their syllabus
  • How AI learns from examples, and why it can be confidently wrong
  • Giving AI clear instructions and a chapter to answer from; turning it into Hinglish voice
  • Connecting several AIs into one system, and testing it like engineers
  • Learn 30 minutes, build 30 minutes, four cycles a day; no coding needed
  • The 3-day version adds a module, a problem statement and a team pitch, with parents if the school allows
  • Classes 8–10 and 11–12 run as separate tracks; 11–12 is more technical
For teachers

Chalk aur Calibration™ — Subject Edition

Teacher wahi. Toolkit nayi.  ·  Department-wise  ·  2–6 hrs per subject

  • Turn an existing chapter into quizzes, flashcards, worksheets and revision material
  • Built around each teacher's own subject — Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Languages, Humanities
  • Output is reusable year after year, reducing fresh preparation
  • No prior technical background assumed

What each side is responsible for

The school provides

  • The computer lab: one working computer per team of 5, plus 2 spare
  • A projector, and internet access to the workshop’s AI websites
  • Two or three consecutive days, 4 hours each, in school hours
  • One or two teachers present in every session
  • Sharing our parent video, flyer and consent form

We handle everything else

  • Program design and all teaching
  • Our build platform, every AI tool and account, headsets and microphones
  • Online payments, receipts, refunds and all parent queries
  • Certificates co-signed by the school, the final-day showcase, highlight video and an outcome report

Teachers are not evaluated on program outcomes, are not held responsible for student performance during sessions, and are not expected to prepare anything beforehand. Students never sign up for any app, never enter personal details into an AI tool, and are photographed only with written parental consent. There is no homework or after-school commitment.

FAQ

Common questions

What age group is this program for? +

Students from Class 9 to Class 12, across all streams. The curriculum focuses on practical AI skills, project building, career exploration and critical thinking about AI usage.

How long is the program? +

Soch aur System runs as a 2-day core or 3-day applied format during school hours. Longer school engagements run 8–10 sessions of 45–60 minutes, typically spread over 4–6 weeks.

Do students need their own laptops? +

No. We work with whatever the school has — a computer lab, tablets, or a projector-based demo setup. The program is designed to run with minimal infrastructure.

Is this safe? Will students misuse AI? +

Safety and ethics are core parts of the curriculum. Students learn responsible usage, how to identify misinformation, and the consequences of misuse. AI is taught as a learning tool, not a shortcut.

What's the cost for parents? +

Fees are comparable to other school activity programs such as robotics or spoken English workshops. Exact pricing depends on batch size and school arrangement. No hidden costs, subscriptions or upsells.

Why should we trust this program? +

It is led by Amaan Shaikh, a working Software Engineer in Germany with a Master's in Computer Science. It is taught by someone who builds AI systems professionally and knows which skills actually matter.
